What I changed and why

“was subject to” → “may include”: the Principle says the final price may be inclusive of Mark Up, so “subject to” is replaced.
“that that”: typo.
“and/or execution of any order linked to or triggered”: this is the Principle’s wording for the order-impact disclosure.
“will” → “may”: the Principle only says Mark Up may impact pricing and execution.
“higher markup” → “a higher Mark Up”: capitalised as a defined term, with the article added.
“discriminates ... based only on sophistication”: the Principle never uses “discriminate” or “sophistication”. It frames fairness through the listed considerations and honest, fair, professional conduct, so I rephrased the conclusion in those terms. If you want to keep the original Annex 1 sentence, this is the one change to reject, since it’s the only place the Annex adds a concept absent from Principle 14.

One point to consider: the Principle also allows different Clients to receive different prices, so the contrast sentence (“In the example below...”) is consistent as it stands.
