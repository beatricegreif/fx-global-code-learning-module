Principle 22: Market Colour

Principle 22 requires you to communicate Market Colour appropriately and without compromising Confidential Information. Market Colour is general information about what is happening in the market, such as broad flows, sentiment and liquidity conditions. Sharing it helps markets work efficiently, but only if it stays general. 
bdo

It matters because the line between harmless colour and confidential information was central to past FX misconduct. Firms are also expected to give staff clear guidance on where that line sits.

Example: A trader tells a client, “We’re seeing steady two-way flow in EUR/USD ahead of the data.” That is Market Colour. Saying “Fund X just sold a large EUR/USD amount for month-end” reveals a client’s identity and trade, so it is Confidential Information and must not be shared.

Question 1
What does Principle 22 of the FX Global Code ask Market Participants to do?

A. Avoid sharing any Market Colour, to eliminate the risk of leaking confidential information
B. Communicate Market Colour appropriately and without compromising Confidential Information
C. Share Market Colour only with counterparties that have signed a Statement of Commitment
D. Share Market Colour only once the relevant trades have been reported

Correct answer: B. Principle 22 says Market Participants should communicate Market Colour appropriately and without compromising Confidential Information. It does not ban Market Colour, and it does not tie sharing to signatory status or trade reporting.

Question 2 (scenario)
A sales trader is asked by a client how the market looks before a central bank decision. Which reply is consistent with Principle 22?

A. “A large pension fund is selling EUR/USD ahead of the decision, and they’re working a big order.”
B. “Our biggest client has stop-losses just below 1.0800, so watch that level.”
C. “It’s been fairly quiet, with a bit more two-way interest in EUR/USD than usual.”
D. “Another bank’s desk told me they’re long GBP and plan to cut the position this afternoon.”

Correct answer: C. It is a general comment on market conditions, which is Market Colour. A, B and D each reveal a specific client’s or counterparty’s identity, orders or positions, which would compromise Confidential Information under Principle 22.

Question 3
According to Principle 22, what should operators of trading platforms that feature tags do when disclosing user information (colour)?

A. Disclose any user information they consider useful to other participants
B. Disclose user information only to participants that pay for premium data
C. Disclose user information only if it has been clearly stated in their disclosure documents, such as rulebooks or guidelines
D. Avoid disclosing any user information, including tag categories

Correct answer: C. Principle 22 says platform operators should only disclose user information (colour) that has been clearly stated in their disclosure documents, so participants know in advance what is shared.
